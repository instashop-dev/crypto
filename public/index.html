A negative min_profit_pct forces demo trades: every scan fills its best cycle even when the edge is negative. The same applies to xchg_min_profit_pct and the best spread — the two gates are independent, so a scan can fill one, both or neither. initial_usdt is immutable — it is the denominator of every P&L figure.

Cross-exchange. Buy a market on the cheaper venue and sell it on the dearer one, in the same instant. Both books are read per scan (Binance over WebSocket, MEXC over REST) and they are not simultaneous — the WS snapshot accumulates over up to 4s while the REST reply is one instant — so timing skew, not depth or fees, is the main source of spreads that were never fillable. Turn xchg_enabled off to restore the triangles-only scan.

India mode. Every leg of a cycle disposes of a VDA — USDT included — so all three attract TDS. Three legs × 1% TDS ≈ 3% of notional per cycle vs ~0.3% in fees, which is why a profitable cycle can still drain the balance. “Net P&L” credits TDS back as a prepayment; “Equity” does not.
